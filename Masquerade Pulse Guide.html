A note for Ray

Ray, sit down.

A pulse is your entry time. That’s the whole secret, and you can stop nodding like you already knew.

Masquerade is The Phantom of the Opera staged across six floors of an old art-supply store on 57th Street. Nobody gets a seat. About 60 of us in masks are led through the opera house, the dressing rooms and the Phantom’s lair while the cast sings inches from our faces.

The building runs that whole show six times a night, fifteen minutes apart, at 7:00, 7:15, 7:30, 7:45, 8:00 and 8:15. Each start time is a pulse. Each pulse gets its own Phantom and its own Christine, and they stay with that one group for the full two hours and change.

So the story, the building and the chandelier are the same for everyone, and the leading man depends on when you walk in. Six emotionally unavailable men in masks, and we only get one. Yes, our type finally has a show.

Before you ask

  • Your ticket is for one entry time. If you book 7:15 and I book 7:45, we see two different casts and wave at each other in the bar afterward.
  • You get a glass of bubbles at the door and a dance lesson soon after. You will do the dance.
  • You may not come dressed as the Phantom. Phantom masks are banned, and the building already has six of him.
J Josh

7:00 Runner-up Pulse 1 of 6 · out about 9:15 PhantomKyle Scatliffe ChristineClaire Leyden The original Phantom and the crossover soprano.

The vibe

The safest pair of hands in the building. Scatliffe has played the 7:00 Phantom since the show opened and is the only one of the original six still in the mask. He is also the Phantom I found the most outside praise for. You’re out by about 9:15 with the whole night ahead of you.

The Phantom

Kyle Scatliffe

At 7:00 since the show opened in 2025

Broadway: Enjolras in the 2014 Les Misérables revival, Harpo in The Color Purple, Lafayette and Jefferson in Hamilton, and Tom Robinson in To Kill a Mockingbird. Olivier Award nominee for The Scottsboro Boys in London.

What people sayThe Amsterdam News critic caught him at a Sunday matinee and called his Phantom extraordinary. On the BroadwayWorld boards, a regular who had also seen Broadway veteran Hugh Panaro named Scatliffe the favorite and said he was captivating and worth the ticket price.

The Christine

Claire Leyden

At 7:00 since August 18, 2026

With the show from the start as a swing and its vocal captain. She first went on as Christine in August 2025 and was promoted to a Christine slot of her own this June. Her background runs from musical theater into concert work and contemporary opera.

What people sayNo critic has reviewed her Christine yet that I could find. Press quoted in her own bio praises a crystal-clear soprano and strong stage presence.

Also cast
Meg is usually Haile Ferrier, the 7:15 Christine. Raoul is shared with 7:45.
Pick it if
You want the most road-tested Phantom, or you want dinner after.
Skip it if
You can’t be on 57th Street by 7:00 sharp. Latecomers are not let in.
7:15 Most modern Pulse 2 of 6 · out about 9:30 PhantomNicholas Edwards ChristineHaile Ferrier The Raoul who got the mask and a Christine with a contemporary mix.

The vibe

On paper, the most contemporary-sounding pulse. Edwards spent most of the show’s first year as Raoul, the rich nice-guy boyfriend, and was promoted to the Phantom at the end of June. Ferrier came up through Dear Evan Hansen and mixes that sound into the soprano writing. If old-school operatic Phantoms feel stiff to you, start here.

The Phantom

Nicholas Edwards

Phantom at 7:15 since June 30, 2026

Masquerade’s original Raoul, and a Phantom understudy before the promotion. Broadway: the original cast of & Juliet as Lord Capulet, and Frozen, where he understudied Kristoff before playing him on tour. He also toured in Les Misérables, covering Enjolras.

What people sayToo new in the mask for reviews. I found none. He does know this staging better than almost anyone, having spent a year watching the Phantom from the other side of the love triangle.

The Christine

Haile Ferrier

At 7:15 since the show opened in 2025

Went straight from college into the first national tour of Dear Evan Hansen, covering Zoe and Alana. Since then: Sarah in Guys and Dolls at Maltz Jupiter Theatre and Sunset Boulevard at the Kennedy Center.

What people sayNew York Theatre Guide’s critic saw her at opening and described her Christine as airy and wide-eyed. On a vocal-coaching podcast she explained that she blends a musical-theater mix with legit soprano, starting bright in “Think of Me” and darkening by “The Point of No Return.”

Also cast
Meg is usually Francesca Mehrotra, the 7:30 Christine. Raoul is shared with 8:00.
Pick it if
You’d rather hear a musical-theater Christine than an operatic one.
Skip it if
You want a Phantom with years in the part. He has had it since June.
7:30 Wild card Pulse 3 of 6 · out about 9:45 PhantomDeven Kolluri ChristineFrancesca Mehrotra The newest Phantom and the opera-trained Christine.

The vibe

The least predictable pulse, with possibly the best-sung Christine. Mehrotra is the classically trained one, and critics noticed. Kolluri is the newest of the six Phantoms. He arrived as a vacation cover in August and, going by this week’s listings, has just taken over this slot from Quentin Earl Darrington. High ceiling, least evidence.

The Phantom

Deven Kolluri

First went on as the Phantom on August 8, 2026

He covered 8:15 for a week in August and this pulse for two weeks into September. BroadwayWorld’s October 9 photo of the six current Phantoms includes him. New Yorkers may know him as Hemant, the groom, in Mira Nair’s Monsoon Wedding musical at St. Ann’s Warehouse in 2023.

What people sayNo reviews yet. A fan wiki records that on August 30 this pulse put up the show’s first all-Desi Phantom, Christine and Raoul.

The Christine

Francesca Mehrotra

A Christine since the show opened in 2025

Opera and operetta on the résumé: Mabel in The Pirates of Penzance at Opera Colorado and Johanna in Sweeney Todd with Pacific Symphony, plus Titanic at Marriott Theatre and A Little Night Music at Ogunquit Playhouse.

What people sayNew York Stage Review covered her pulse at opening, opposite Hugh Panaro, and singled out how beautifully she sang the role. She has said some scenes are sung without amplification, and she credits her classical training for carrying them.

Also cast
Meg is usually Kaley Ann Voorhees, the Broadway Christine from 7:45, in the smaller part. Raoul is Dan Hoy.
Pick it if
The soprano matters most to you, or you like seeing someone before everyone else catches on.
Skip it if
You want certainty about who is in the mask. This slot has changed Phantoms more than any other this year.
7:45 Top pick Pulse 4 of 6 · out about 10:00 PhantomDerrick Davis ChristineKaley Ann Voorhees The tour Phantom and the Broadway Christine.

The vibe

The only pulse where both leads have already spent years in these exact roles. Davis led the national tour as the Phantom. Voorhees was Christine at the Majestic on Broadway. This is the big, classic, full-voiced version, sung an arm’s length away by two people who know every bar of it. For a first visit, it’s the one.

The Phantom

Derrick Davis

At 7:45 since May 11, 2026

He first put on the mask in October 2016 at Bass Performance Hall in Fort Worth, taking over the title role on the national tour, and came back in 2019 for the tour’s final stretch. Before that he logged more than 1,000 Broadway performances in The Lion King, where he was the first actor to cover both Mufasa and Scar. Phantom was the first show he ever saw.

What people sayTour critics were loud about him. The Las Vegas Review-Journal’s reviewer, a repeat Phantom-goer, wrote that Davis was the first to make him feel the character’s heartbreak and side with the Phantom over Raoul. A Los Angeles critic said he beat high expectations. Those are reviews of the tour. Nothing has been published on his Masquerade Phantom yet.

The Christine

Kaley Ann Voorhees

At 7:45 since the show opened in 2025

Made her Broadway debut as Christine in 2014 at age 20, the youngest ever in the New York production, and took over the role full-time in 2018. Also Prince of Broadway, Candide with New York City Opera, and Christine again on the international tour.

What people sayThe Daily Beast’s Tim Teeman saw this pulse at opening and wrote that the score was beautifully sung. BroadwayBox once asked her which Drag Race queens she would cast as Christine and Carlotta, so she has fielded our kind of question before.

Also cast
Meg is usually Riley Noland, the 8:00 Christine. Raoul is shared with 7:00.
Pick it if
It’s your first time, or the singing matters most to you.
Skip it if
You’ve seen the classic version plenty and want something stranger. Try 8:15.

If you want a voice built for huge touring houses aimed at you from a few feet away, this is the pulse. Cartell sang Jean Valjean on the Les Misérables tour more than 1,200 times. Noland has been the 8:00 Christine since the show opened and drew one of the warmest individual notices I found.

At 8:00 since July 21, 2026

The Phantom

Ryan Vona

In the show since January 2026

Broadway: Once, where he played Andrej and several instruments, a lead role in Cirque du Soleil’s Paramour, Beautiful, and standby for Leo Frank in the 2023 Parade revival.

What people sayA BroadwayWorld regular who saw him in February loved the youth of it. Before you get dressed

House rules

Dress
Black, white or silver. The show says formal is encouraged. In this group it’s mandatory. Turn up off-code and they lend you a cloak, and everyone will know why you’re wearing it.
Mask
Required, in the same three colors. Bring your own or they hand you one at the door. No full-face masks, no LEDs and no Phantom masks.
Shoes
No stilettos. There are escalators, up to thirty steps between floors and about two hours on your feet. If stairs are a problem, there is an elevator route, but it has to be arranged with the show in advance.
Getting in
A password arrives by email 24 hours before. Everyone in the party needs it, plus photo ID.
Timing
Doors open at your entry time and latecomers are not admitted. Be on 57th Street fifteen minutes early.
Bags, phones
Coats and bags are checked for free. No photos or recording during the show. Save it for the Lake Bar at the end, where photos are allowed.
Food, drink
Bubbles on arrival, water along the way and a full bar at the end. No intermission, so eat first. The show’s own suggestions within a block are Trattoria Dell’Arte, Paris Bar and Brooklyn Diner.
December
Some scenes play on the roof when the weather allows. In the cold they move indoors, so we may not get the skyline.
Effects
Haze and strobe lights throughout.
